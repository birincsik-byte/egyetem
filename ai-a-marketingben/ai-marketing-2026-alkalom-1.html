Birincsik Albert

A tárgy

Miért vagyunk itt?

Hogy megtanuld, hogyan dolgozik egy marketinges AI-jal.

Nem helyetted gondolkodik

Gyorsabban és többet tudsz vele kipróbálni.

A félév végére

egy teljes márkabevezetést viszel végig, az ötlettől a kész kampányanyagokig.

Ami a legfontosabb

Az AI nagy segítség, de mindent, amit ad, értelmezni kell, felülbírálni és kritikusan szemlélni.

Értelmezed
Javítod
Kritikával nézed

Nem attól leszel jó szakember, hogy egy az egyben elfogadod, amit kapsz. Nem arra vagyok kíváncsi, milyen gyorsan tudsz dolgozni. Itt a hatékonyság a minőséget és a mélységet jelenti.

Mit csinálunk

Marketing tender: valós márka egy elképzelt iparágban

Például: mi lenne, ha a Coca-Cola autómárka lenne?

Megtartod

A márka nevét, logóját, személyiségét, értékeit és vizuális kódjait.

→

Áthelyezed

Egy elképzelt új iparágba. Kitalálod, mit kínálna ott, kinek és hogyan.

→

Elindítod

Megtervezed és elkészíted a bevezető kampányát.

Órai játék: a valódi márka nevével és logójával dolgozunk, de csak itt, az órán. A kész anyagokat ne tedd közzé, mert a márka nem a tiéd. Egyéni munka, mindenki a választott márkával dolgozik.

Min fogsz dolgozni

Egy ügynökség egy tenderen. A karmester te vagy.

Ez egy szimuláció, ami a valós életet modellezi: így dolgozik egy ügynökség, amikor meg akar nyerni egy ügyfelet. Egy tenderen ezek a szakemberek dolgoznak együtt. Te ezt egymagad csinálod meg, a kompetenciákat az AI segítségével oldod meg.

Az elvárt kimenet

Egy tenderdeck, amit óráról órára építesz fel

15-20 dia

Tenderdeck az új márkáról és a bevezető kampányáról, rögzített szerkezet szerint.

Prototípusok

A deck mögött: arculat, weboldal, posztok, videóspot és a többi.

15-20 perc

2026. december 10-én bemutatod a decket, és elmeséled, hogyan készült.

Mintha ügynökségként az ügyfélnek vinnéd, és meg akarnád nyerni a tendert. A bemutatón azt is elmondod, mit csináltál, mivel, mire jöttél rá, és hol voltak az AI korlátai.

A tenderdeck szerkezete

Öt blokk

1

Bevezetés

Címlap névvel, logóval és szlogennel. A probléma, amit a márka megold. A megoldás és az egyedi értékajánlat.

2

Stratégia

Célcsoport és 2 persona. Hol áll a márka a versenytársakhoz képest. Márkaszemélyiség, hangnem, arculat.

3

Kampánykoncepció

A nagy ötlet és a kampányszlogen. A bevezetés három fázisa: teaser, launch, sustain.

4

Kreatív bemutató

Kulcsvizuálok, posztok, bannerek, plakát, csomagolás, weboldal-prototípus, videóspot, hírlevél.

5

Csatornák

Melyik kreatív melyik csatornán fut: paid, earned, owned.

Minden, amit a hat alkalmon készítesz, ebbe a deckbe kerül. A blokkok aránya rajtad múlik. A kreatív anyagok prototípusok: valódi gyártás, éles kampány és védjegybejegyzés nem része a feladatnak.

Strukturált
Szerepkör: Te egy tapasztalt márkastratéga vagy egy ügynökségnél.
Feladat: elemezd a [kiinduló márka] márkát.
Scope: csak a márka identitása érdekel, a pénzügyi adatok nem.
Elvárt kimenet: táblázat ezekkel a sorokkal: értékek, személyiség (5 jelző), hangnem, vizuális kódok (színek, formák, betűk), 3 ikonikus kampány. Minden sorhoz forráslink.

Szabad szöveggel, ahogy egy kollégának mondanád
Szia! Egy egyetemi projekten dolgozom, amiben egy ismert márkából indítok egy teljesen új márkát egy másik kategóriában. A kiinduló márkám: [kiinduló márka], az új kategória: [új kategória]. Tehát nem a [kiinduló márka] fog [új kategória] terméket kiadni, hanem az a kérdés, hogy ha ez a márka a személyiségével, az értékeivel és a stílusával egy [új kategória] márka lenne, milyen lenne.

Ahhoz, hogy ezt jól csináljam, először pontosan értenem kell, mitől az a [kiinduló márka], ami. Kérlek, viselkedj úgy, mint egy tapasztalt márkastratéga, aki évek óta ilyen márkákat elemez, és segíts összerakni egy elemzést róla: mik az értékei, milyen a személyisége (ha ember lenne, milyen ember lenne), hogyan beszél a közönségével, mik a felismerhető vizuális kódjai (színek, formák, betűk, képi világ), és melyek voltak azok a kampányai, amik ezt a legjobban megmutatták.

The course

Why are we here?

To learn how a marketer works with AI.

It does not think for you

It lets you try more things, faster.

By the end of the semester

you will have taken a complete brand launch from idea to finished campaign materials.

What matters most

AI is a great help, but everything it gives you has to be interpreted, overruled where needed and looked at critically.

Interpret
Correct
Question

You do not become a good professional by accepting what you get as it is. I am not interested in how fast you can work. Here, efficiency means quality and depth.

What we do

Marketing pitch: a real brand in an imagined industry

For example: what if Coca-Cola were a car brand?

You keep

The brand's name, logo, personality, values and visual codes.

→

You move it

Into an imagined new industry. You work out what it would offer there, to whom and how.

→

You launch

You plan and produce its launch campaign.

A classroom game: we work with the real brand's name and logo, but only here, in class. Do not publish the finished materials, because the brand is not yours. Individual work, everyone works with the brand they chose.

What you will work on

An agency on a pitch. You are the conductor.

This is a simulation that models real life: this is how an agency works when it wants to win a client. On a pitch, these specialists work together. You do all of it on your own, covering these competencies with the help of AI.

What is expected

A pitch deck you build session by session

15-20 slides

A pitch deck of the new brand and its launch campaign, along a fixed structure.

Prototypes

Behind the deck: identity, website, posts, video spot and the rest.

15-20 minutes

On 10 December 2026 you present the deck and tell the story of how it was made.

As if you were an agency bringing it to the client to win the pitch. In the presentation you also tell what you did, with what, what you learned, and where the limits of AI were.

The structure of the pitch deck

Five blocks

1

Introduction

Title slide with name, logo and tagline. The problem the brand solves. The solution and the unique value proposition.

2

Strategy

Target audience and 2 personas. Where the brand stands against competitors. Brand personality, tone of voice, identity.

3

Campaign concept

The big idea and the campaign slogan. The three launch phases: teaser, launch, sustain.

4

Creative showcase

Key visuals, posts, banners, billboard, packaging, website prototype, video spot, newsletter.

5

Channels

Which creative runs on which channel: paid, earned, owned.

Everything you make in the six sessions goes into this deck. How you balance the blocks is up to you. The creative materials are prototypes: real production, a live campaign and trademark registration are not part of the assignment.

A good prompt is like a good brief

Whoever receives it can do the job without asking questions.

1
Role

Whose head it should think with. "You are an experienced brand strategist at an agency."

2
Context

What it needs to know. Paste in or upload your materials so far.

3
Task

In one sentence, what it has to do. One prompt, one task.

4
Scope

What is in and what is out.

5
Expected output

Table, list, one page, HTML, SVG.

6
Parameters

Quantity, length, tone, language, audience, style. For images aspect ratio, light, colours with HEX codes.

7
Criteria

What makes the result good, what it should avoid.

8
Example

If you have a sample, give it.

9
Let it ask

At the end: "If anything is missing, ask first."

Prompting

A prompt template you can fill in for every task

Template
Role: You are a [specialist] at an agency.
Context: [the source brand and the new category, the materials so far, the target audience]
Task: [in one sentence]
Scope: [what is in, what is out]
Expected output: [format]
Parameters: [quantity, length, tone, language, audience]
Criteria: [what makes it good, what to avoid]
If anything is missing, ask first.

Tips: break the big task into steps, ask for several variants, give specific feedback, and ask it to rate its own answer against your criteria. Do not put personal or confidential data in it.

Session 1The game

What if…

Choose a brand. Everyone gets a different one.

We use the brand differently from reality: its name and logo stay, only the industry is new. The question is what it would be like if, with its personality, values and style, it worked in a completely different industry. It is a classroom game; it does not exist in reality.

Session 1If you do not know how to start

Prompt suggestion: Brand analysis

Tools for this: For research and analysis Claude or ChatGPT with web search on, Perplexity, Gemini; for the semester plan any assistant; for organising sources NotebookLM.

Structured
Role: You are an experienced brand strategist at an agency.
Task: analyse the [source brand] brand.
Scope: I am only interested in the brand's identity, not its financial data.
Expected output: a table with these rows: values, personality (5 adjectives), tone of voice, visual codes (colours, shapes, type), 3 iconic campaigns. A source link for every row.
Parameters: in English, at most 2 sentences per row.
Criteria: where you cannot find a source, write that there is no source.
In free text, the way you would say it to a colleague
Hi! I am working on a university project in which I start a completely new brand in another category, based on a well-known brand. My source brand: [source brand], the new category: [new category]. So it is not [source brand] launching a [new category] product; the question is what this brand would be like if, with its personality, values and style, it were a [new category] brand.

To do this well, I first need to understand exactly what makes [source brand] what it is. Please act like an experienced brand strategist who has been analysing brands like this for years, and help me put together an analysis of it: what its values are, what its personality is like (if it were a person, what kind of person would it be), how it talks to its audience, what its recognisable visual codes are (colours, shapes, type, imagery), and which of its campaigns showed this best.

Important: only write what you can back with a source, and add the link. If you are not sure about something, say so, do not make it up. I am not interested in its financial data, only the brand itself. Write in English, concisely, in a table, so I can work from it easily later.

Before you start, ask if anything is unclear.

Session 1If you do not know how to start

Prompt suggestion: Semester timeline and competitors

Semester timeline
Here is the assignment description for the semester [pasted or uploaded]. I am building a [new category] brand in the spirit of [source brand]. Make me a timeline for the six sessions: what gets done in class, what is left for home, and which material goes into which block of the deck. In a table, and flag where you see a bottleneck.
Competitors
Find 5 real [new category] brands on the [country or region] market. In a table: offer, price level, tone of voice, main channels, source link. Only list existing companies, and mark where you are unsure.

Fill in the parts in square brackets with your own project details.

Session 1Tips and advice · 1/3

Account and interface

1

One task, one chat

Open a separate conversation for every sub-task and name it, for example "Brand analysis". You will find it again at the end of the semester, and you will need it for the making-of story.

2

Web search on

Switch it on before you research. Without it the AI answers from its own knowledge, which can be outdated or inaccurate.

3

Find out today what your account can do

Try file upload, creating documents and presentations, and check whether Claude Design and Claude Slides are available to you. If something is missing, say so today.

4

One folder for the semester

Create a folder on your computer or in the cloud and save everything you produce. A chat is not an archive.

Session 1Tips and advice · 2/3

Research with AI

1

Open the sources

Open every link. If it does not exist or says something other than what the AI claims, delete the claim.

2

Two tools, one question

Ask the same question to two AIs, for example Claude and ChatGPT. Where the answers differ, that is where you need to check.

3

The competitor's own site

Check the offer, price level and tone of voice on the competitor's own site and channels. Base the price level on real prices, not estimates.

4

See it with your own eyes

Do not just read about the visual codes: look at the brand's site, ads and packaging, and compare them with what the AI wrote.

Session 1Tips and advice · 3/3

Think ahead

1

A making-of log from today

Note which tool and prompt you used, what worked and where the AI was wrong. In session 6 you tell the story of the deck from this.

2

The paragraph is yours

Write what you carry over from the source brand and what you do not yourself first, and only then ask the AI to critique it. The brand platform, the identity and the campaign build on it.

3

A realistic timeline

For the timeline, tell the AI how much time you have outside class, and ask it to flag bottlenecks. Look at session 5 separately; a lot comes together there.

4

If you run out of usage

Continue the text work in ChatGPT, keep Claude for the final output, or switch to any other tool. Instead of a long chat, start a new one with a short summary.
